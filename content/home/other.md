I was the drummer between 1991 and 1995 of [Kactus Jack](https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kactus_Jack).
 You can see the original [unofficial home page](Kactus/Welcome.html),
 vintage style. We recorded several <a href="https://www.discogs.com/artist/1311439-Kactus-Jack">disks</a>.

Before that, I was the drummer of <a href="http://no80s-gruposnacionales.blogspot.com.es/2009/01/los-murcielagos.html">Los Murciélagos</a> for a short period (we won the <i>Concurso de Maquetas</i>) and <a href="https://www.discogs.com/artist/5529204-Abor%C3%ACgenes">Los Aborígenes</a> (we won the <i>Concurso Interbarrios de Rock Ciudad de Oviedo</i> which enabled us to be part of a record).
